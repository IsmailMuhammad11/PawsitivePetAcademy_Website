About Pawsitive Pet Academy

Education that improves the wellbeing of pets and creates opportunities for people.

Our Story

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 by Sarah Smith to provide pet care education and practical animal training for pet owners, aspiring pet care professionals and animal lovers. Sarah noticed that many people own pets but know little about proper pet care, training, nutrition and emergency care, so she built an academy that develops practical skills while creating employment opportunities in the pet care industry.

Our Track Record

Based in Durban, hundreds of pet owners and aspiring professionals have completed our six-month Professional Development Programmes and six-week Practical Skills Courses.

Who We Teach

Individuals, families, pet sitters, kennel staff, veterinary assistants and aspiring entrepreneurs.
