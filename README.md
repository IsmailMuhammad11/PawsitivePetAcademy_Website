 About the project

Pawsitive Pet Academy is a pet care education and animal training academy in Durban, founded by Sarah Smith in 2023. This website advertises the academy's courses, lets visitors build a course quote with automatic discounts, and lets them send an enquiry. The design follows the client's brief: caring, trustworthy and professional.

 Features

- Drop-down menu: the Courses menu lists all seven courses, grouped as six-month programmes and six-week short courses. It works with hover, click and keyboard, and on mobile.
- Tables: a course comparison table, a fee table with checkboxes, and discount tier tables.
- Fee and discount calculator (JavaScript): subtotal, discount and total update live as courses are selected.
  - 1 course: no discount
  - 2 courses: 5%
  - 3 courses: 10%
  - More than 3 courses: 15%
- Quote saved between pages: courses added on a course page appear on the Fees and Contact pages.
- Error handling: the contact form validates name, email, South African phone number and message, with inline error messages and an error banner. Storage and submission are wrapped in try/catch, and there is a custom 404 page.
- Responsive design: works on desktop, tablet and mobile, with a mobile menu.
